## Robustness across random seeds (6 tasks a day)

The random misses and session patterns change with the seed, so the cadence gaps wobble by a few days. Longest gap counting level-ups, then counting only unlocks, trophies and ranks:

| Seed | Month 1 (target 2) | Months 2–12 (target 7) | Years 2–5 (target 14) | 10M aura | 100M aura |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 · 4 | 11 · 14 | 9 · 55 | month 18.9 | month 41.3 |
| 3 | 3 · 3 | 8 · 15 | 9 · 60 | month 17.4 | month 40.2 |
| 5 | 4 · 4 | 8 · 17 | 11 · 55 | month 17.0 | month 42.9 |
| 7 (default) | 2 · 2 | 7 · 12 | 10 · 67 | month 16.8 | month 39.8 |

In later years the aura-based rewards are geometric while her growth is steady, so between garden unlocks the steady drumbeat comes from level-ups (each one is a celebrated moment with its own card), the "Days in the garden" trophies and project milestones.

Rank ladders for reference: martial 17 ranks, college 10 ranks, swapnserve 8 ranks, hurley 7 ranks, custom 7 ranks.
